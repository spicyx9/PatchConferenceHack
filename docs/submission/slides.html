Conferge

Conferences are organised by time slot, not by what you came for.

  • GitHub Universe: 2 days, 161 sessions, 231 side events found, thousands of people.
  • The people who matter to you stay invisible.
  • Most attendees go home feeling they were in the wrong room.

What Conferge does

One sentence in. Who to meet, where, and why, out.

Goal"Meet seed investors, AI agent founders, Copilot PMs" becomes three weighted goals.
Who to meet, and whyPeople ranked against your goals. The why is a path in the graph, with its sources.
Plan and mapDay by day for the days you are in town. Official sessions and side events together.
Anti-FOMOTwo events at once: which one, why, and what you give up.
Live replanningAn event sells out. The plan is recomputed, the diff explains the swap.
Memory"I met this person" is written to the graph. The ranking changes now and stays for the next conference.

  • One graph for the planner, the replanner and your memory. Same ids, shared state between agents.
  • Replanning is one traversal: who else is at the sold-out event, where else do they appear. Writes only through GraphStore MERGE.

Bright Data, the acquisition layer

Data a plain request cannot reach, labelled by freshness.

Collected live today, public endpoints

  • Official catalog, Luma, Partiful, Eventbrite, party aggregators
  • BART fares, OSRM routes
  • Every record: source URL, collected_at, source_mode

Behind one Bright Data client

  • SERP discovery for the long tail of side events
  • Google Flights, Booking and LinkedIn datasets, Web Unlocker for pages that block
  • No key: the connector says missing_credentials, the UI shows a fixture badge. Nothing invented.

The header badge reads fixture or live. We say on stage exactly what it says.
