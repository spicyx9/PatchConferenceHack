# Positioning (6 sentences, from docs/knowledge/14-market-landscape.md)

1. "AI conference assistant" is a solved category: organizer platforms such as Swapcard
   Sherlock, Grip or Brella already recommend people and sessions, but only inside one event,
   on the organizer's data, with no travel and no external side events.
2. Conferge is the opportunity optimizer for a business trip: independent of the organizer,
   cross-platform, and it starts from the professional outcome you want, not from the agenda.
3. It builds one attendee-centric opportunity graph across platforms, linking people,
   companies, side events, talks, topics, venues and weighted goals.
4. It treats the side event as a networking object, using hosts and co-hosts, which none of
   the aggregators or attendee networking tools we studied do.
5. Reverse planning turns weighted goals into a plan, people, travel and budget, with an
   explainable trade-off between an official talk, a Luma party and a dinner, including
   distance and who you miss.
6. The differentiation is the combination, not any single brick, and it points toward
   attendee ROI that ties spend to meetings and opportunities.
